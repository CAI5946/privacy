FlowStrength Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FlowStrength, provided by CAI, handles information when you use the app.

1. What the app stores

FlowStrength is designed to store your workout-related data primarily on your device. This may include workout records, training plans, exercise library data, progress statistics, memos, and app preferences, as well as AI conversations, AI user instructions, and AI memory. The app does not require an account and does not offer cloud sync.

2. AI assistant and data processing

When you use the built-in AI assistant, the app sends your message, together with the minimum training context needed to answer that question (which may include workout records, training plans, personal notes, and the user instructions and memory you have saved), to Google's Gemini API via Firebase AI Logic to generate a response. Data is transmitted in encrypted form. Google processes this data solely as a processor to generate responses.

Before you use the AI assistant for the first time, the app shows a data usage notice and requires your confirmation.

When the assistant uses web search, your search query is sent to Google to retrieve web sources, and the response may display source links.

The app sends a device integrity attestation result to Google via Firebase App Check to help prevent abuse of the AI service.

In the current release, the AI cannot create, modify, or delete your workout data.

3. Voice input and microphone

The AI input box supports voice input. When you tap the microphone button, the app requests microphone permission and uses your device's system speech recognition service to convert speech into text. Depending on your device, the system recognition service may process audio over the internet. The transcribed text only fills the input box and is not sent to the AI assistant until you manually send it. You can deny microphone permission at any time; voice input being unavailable does not affect other app features.

4. Notifications

The app may request notification permission so it can show workout or timer-related notifications. You can deny or disable this permission in your device settings at any time.

5. Backup import and export

If you choose to export a backup, the app creates a backup file and lets you share or save it using the options available on your device. If you choose to import a backup, the app reads the file you select in order to restore your data. AI conversations, user instructions, and memory are not included in backup files.

6. Data sharing

FlowStrength does not sell your personal data and does not use advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs. Except in the following cases, your data does not leave your device: (a) when you use the AI assistant, your message and the limited training context described above are sent to Google (Gemini API); (b) device integrity attestation information is sent to Google via Firebase App Check; (c) when web search is used, the search query is sent to Google.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because app data is primarily stored on your device, you can remove it by clearing app data, deleting the app, or deleting any backup files you exported. You can delete AI conversations from the AI history, and delete or disable user instructions and memory in the AI settings. AI data is stored only on your device and is removed by clearing app data or deleting the app.

8. Children

FlowStrength is not specifically directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information in a way that requires action, contact us.

9. Medical disclaimer

FlowStrength is a fitness recording tool only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or prevention advice.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: 5946jiale@gmail.com

隐私政策（简体中文）

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 17 日

本隐私政策说明由 CAI 提供的 FlowStrength 在你使用本应用时如何处理信息。

1. 应用存储的信息

FlowStrength 主要将你的训练相关数据保存在你的设备本地。这些数据可能包括训练记录、训练计划、动作库数据、进度统计、备忘录、应用偏好设置，以及 AI 对话记录、AI 使用说明（User Instructions）和 AI 记忆（Memory）。本应用不需要注册或登录账号，不提供云同步。

2. AI 助手与数据处理

当你使用内置 AI 助手时，应用会将你的消息，以及为回答该问题所需的最小范围训练上下文（可能包括训练记录、训练计划、个人备注、你保存的使用说明和记忆），通过 Firebase AI Logic 发送给 Google 的 Gemini API 以生成回答。传输过程经过加密。Google 仅作为受托处理方为生成回答处理这些数据。

首次使用 AI 助手前，应用会显示数据用途说明，并需要你的确认。

当回答使用网页搜索时，你的搜索查询会发送给 Google 以检索网页来源，回答中可能展示来源链接。

应用通过 Firebase App Check 向 Google 发送设备完整性证明结果，用于防止 AI 服务被滥用。

当前正式版本中，AI 不能创建、修改或删除你的训练数据。

3. 语音输入与麦克风

AI 输入框支持语音输入。点击麦克风按钮时，应用会请求麦克风权限，并使用设备系统的语音识别服务将语音转为文字。取决于你的设备，系统识别服务可能通过联网处理音频。转写文字只会填入输入框，你手动发送前不会发送给 AI 助手。你可以随时拒绝麦克风权限；语音输入不可用不影响应用其他功能。

4. 通知权限

本应用可能会请求通知权限，用于显示训练或计时相关通知。你可以随时在设备系统设置中拒绝或关闭该权限。

5. 备份导入与导出

当你主动导出备份时，应用会创建备份文件，并通过设备提供的方式让你自行保存或分享。当你主动导入备份时，应用会读取你选择的文件以恢复数据。AI 对话、使用说明和记忆不包含在备份文件中。

6. 数据共享

FlowStrength 不出售你的个人数据，不使用广告 SDK 或第三方统计 SDK。除以下情形外，你的数据不会离开你的设备：(a) 使用 AI 助手时，你的消息和上述有界训练上下文发送至 Google（Gemini API）；(b) 设备完整性证明信息通过 Firebase App Check 发送至 Google；(c) 使用网页搜索时，搜索查询发送至 Google。

7. 数据保留与删除

由于应用数据主要保存在你的设备本地，你可以通过清除应用数据、卸载应用或删除你导出的备份文件来移除相关数据。你可以在 AI 历史中删除对话，在 AI 设置中删除或停用使用说明和记忆。AI 数据仅保存在设备本地，清除应用数据或卸载应用即被移除。

8. 儿童隐私

FlowStrength 并非专门面向 13 岁以下儿童设计。如果你认为儿童以需要处理的方式提供了信息，请联系我们。

9. 医疗免责声明

FlowStrength 仅为健身记录工具，不是医疗器械，也不提供医疗诊断、治疗或预防建议。
